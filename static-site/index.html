How To Use The Blamp

01

Charge it, clip it on

A 90-minute USB-C charge gives you up to 10 hours of light. Clip the lobster clasp to a handbag, clutch, backpack, keys or a pet collar.

02

Tap once to light

One tap turns it on or off. Hold for 16 seconds to keep it lit, and tap through three brightness levels.

03

See everything, inside and out

Find your keys at the bottom of your bag, light the lock at the front door, or unclip it and carry it for the walk home.
