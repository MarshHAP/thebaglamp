How To Use The Blamp

01

Clip it in

Charge with the USB-C cable, then clip The Blamp to the inside lining of your bag, facing down towards your things.

02

Reach in, light on

The motion sensor wakes the lamp the moment your hand goes in. A soft, warm light shows you everything at once.

03

Take it out

Double-tap to lock it on and use it as a torch — for the front door, the car park, or the walk home.
